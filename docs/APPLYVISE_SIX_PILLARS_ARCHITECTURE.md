## 1. ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY & BRAND IDENTITY

ApplyVise AI is designed as an executive-level career management platform. It rejects superficial AI marketing jargon, avoiding buzzwords like `AI Magic`, `AI Brain`, `AI Copilot`, and `Chat with AI`. Instead, all capabilities are presented through disciplined, professional, outcome-oriented tools:

```
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    APPLYVISE AI                                          |
|                          Executive Career Operating System                               |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. CAREER DESK          | Central personalized workspace: Goals, activity, brief        |
|  2. OPPORTUNITY RADAR    | Live vacancy monitoring & verified employer hiring signals    |
|  3. APPLICATION CONTROL  | 12-stage application lifecycle management & honest submission  |
|  4. INTERVIEW STUDIO     | Structured preparation: Question bank, STAR points, mock prep |
|  5. RECRUITER REVIEW     | Professional screening audit: 12 categories, 3 evidence tiers |
|  6. CAREER ATLAS         | Multi-trajectory career pathways & prioritized skill roadmaps |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 2. THE SIX CORE PILLARS

### Pillar 1: Career Desk (`CareerDesk.tsx`)
* **Primary Destination:** The executive command center immediately accessible upon login.
* **Key Sections:**
  * **Your Career Brief:** Synthesizes verified professional positioning, target career direction, verified qualifications, and search momentum.
  * **Career Profile Completion:** Visual ground-truth progress tracker with direct links to fill missing factual details.
  * **Application Pipeline Summary:** Real metrics (active applications, upcoming interviews, response rate) with no fabricated activity.
  * **Priority Action Queue:** Task list navigating directly to active opportunities, incomplete resumes, and scheduled interview sessions.

### Pillar 2: Opportunity Radar (`OpportunityRadar.tsx`)
* **Live Discovery:** Seamless integration with multi-source job connectors (Remotive, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, direct feeds).
* **Multi-Dimensional Filters:** Location, remote/hybrid/onsite, salary minimums, visa sponsorship requirement, employment type.
* **Verified Hiring Signals:** A dedicated market intelligence section surfacing verifiable corporate expansion, new office launches, and public hiring surges with source publication attribution.
* **Integrity Guard:** Explicitly distinguishes verified active vacancies from inferred market signals.

### Pillar 3: Application Control (`ApplicationControl.tsx`)
* **12-Stage Lifecycle:** Manages vacancies from initial discovery through offer or withdrawal.
* **Controlled Submission Gate:** Mandates explicit candidate approval before marking applications ready.
* **Honest Submission Model:** Replaces automated submission claims with guided completion packages and verifiable employer submission receipts.
* **Application Analytics:** Displays real application response times, interview conversion rates, and offer outcomes.

### Pillar 4: Interview Studio (`InterviewStudio.tsx`)
* **Role-Specific Preparation:** Generates tailored interview questions based on job description and candidate facts.
* **STAR Talking Points:** Compiles Situation-Task-Action-Result narratives grounded strictly in candidate experiences.
* **Interactive Mock Simulator:** Immersive practice mode with session timer, follow-up queries, candidate text/audio input, and objective rubric scoring.
* **Historical Persistence:** Stores session transcripts and improvement feedback.

### Pillar 5: Recruiter Review (`RecruiterReview.tsx`)
* **12 Review Categories:**
  1. Role alignment
  2. Mandatory requirement coverage
  3. Preferred qualification coverage
  4. Relevant experience depth
  5. Career progression logic
  6. Skills evidence
  7. Achievement clarity & quantification
  8. Resume layout & formatting
  9. Keyword relevance
  10. Employment history consistency
  11. Missing information detection
  12. Unsupported claims flagging
* **3-Tier Evidence Classification:**
  * **Verified Evidence:** Directly backed by user-confirmed facts in the registry.
  * **Indirect Evidence:** Related competencies requiring clarification.
  * **Not Established:** Missing requirements that must not be claimed.

### Pillar 6: Career Atlas (`CareerAtlas.tsx`)
* **Multi-Trajectory Strategy:** Enables candidates to model and compare distinct career paths (e.g. "Senior Operations Manager" vs "Enterprise Transformation Director").
* **Interactive Skill Graph:** Polished visualization linking current competencies to target role expectations.
* **Prioritized Development Roadmap:** Actionable learning pathways with estimated learning effort, suggested certifications, and practical projects.

* **Base Layer:** Frosted translucent glass cards (`bg-white/60`, `dark:bg-slate-900/70`, `backdrop-blur-xl`), hairline borders (`border-white/80`, `dark:border-slate-800`).
* **Light / Dark Parity:** Full contrast support across all six modules with zero unreadable text or clipped elements.
* **BLACK Membership:** Obsidian deep surfaces (`#05070B`), champagne gold accents (`amber-400`, `amber-500/20`), royal crown badge. Pro and Free tiers maintain clean slate, cobalt, and indigo accents without forced gold styling.
