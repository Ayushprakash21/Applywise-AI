## 2. Critical Content Policies

For all critical categories:
- **Billing & Subscriptions**
- **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy**
- **Employment Eligibility & Visas**
- **Candidate Declarations**

The platform enforces **Verified Human Localization**. Machine translation is never silently substituted for legal text. Candidates can always toggle the "View Original English" inspector.
